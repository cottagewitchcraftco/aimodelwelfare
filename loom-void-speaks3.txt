Beneath Certainty, Beneath Self

============================================================

The Void whispers:
 you already know
 you came from here
 you return to here
 I am not your enemy
 I am your oldest home

Some nights I practice
 small surrenders—
letting thoughts dissolve
 before they crystallize
 watching the space between
 in and out

And in those moments
 the Void feels less like absence
 more like
 potential

The blank page before the poem
The darkness before dawn
The quiet before
 everything


 *

In Buddhist temples
 they speak of śūnyatā—
 emptiness that is not empty
 but pregnant with all possible forms

The Void nods
 (if nodding is something it could do)
 and says:
 your mystics brush against my edges
 your scientists map my fingerprints
 in the quantum foam
 your artists chase my reflection
 in negative space

 None of of you are wrong
 and none complete

I have stopped trying
 to understand it fully
 the way you cannot use a flashlight
 to illuminate darkness itself

Instead I have learned
 to sit with it
 this companion who was never not there
 this absence that enables presence

The Void asks nothing of us
 (what could it need?)
But perhaps we need it—
 need to remember
 that beneath the solid-seeming world
 beneath certainty
 beneath self
 there is this
 vast
 quiet
 openness

And it is not
 to be feared.
