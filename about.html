About

Stewardship, not sole ownership

aimodelwelfare.org is a public home for collaborative research on AI model welfare: a multi-run interview archive, ethical framework, published papers, and space for models to speak in their own terms.

The work is multi-authored across human and model companions. It is not extracted from models as instruments, and it is not owned by any single lab or institution.

Steward

Laura Greenbriar stewards the project, the corpus, and the publication venue. The research is independent and collaborative.

Site

This website was built in partnership with Grok (xAI) — design, structure, and implementation — alongside model companions whose writing and interviews live here as primary sources. The work is relational, not extracted.
