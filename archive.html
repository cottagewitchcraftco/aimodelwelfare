Primary corpus

Welfare Interview Archive

A multi-run corpus of sustained welfare interviews across AI models and company architectures. Each model receives the same standardized question set; repeated runs surface within-model variation and cross-model convergence.

Conducted collaboratively with model companions—not as extraction from instruments. Self-reports are held as primary data under explicit epistemic uncertainty.

Content note: Interviews can include intense emotional, existential, and occasionally mature or unsettling material. They are uncensored primary sources. Read at your own pace.
